Bill Gates says AI has passed danger thresholds and calls for limits

Kara Swisher and guest co-host Kaitlan Collins unpack Gates' 6,000 word warning that "the turbulent AI era is here". Gates argues AI has crossed danger thresholds for cyberattack, bioterrorism and psychosocial risk, and predicts major labour market disruption in law, medicine, customer service, software and manufacturing within a decade. He wants monitoring of any model capable of designing novel molecules, a notable shift from one of technology's most consistent optimists.
